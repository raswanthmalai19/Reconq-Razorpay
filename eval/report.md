## What This Comparison Shows
Both systems share the identical trained confidence model. The only difference is the decision policy: ReconQ requires higher confidence as the transaction amount grows; the naive baseline applies one fixed cutoff regardless of amount. Any divergence in the false-match rate above is attributable entirely to that policy difference, not to a different underlying model.
